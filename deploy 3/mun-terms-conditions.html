These terms govern your registration and participation in the YSP Winter Model United Nations 2026 conference. Please read them before confirming your seat — confirmation constitutes acceptance.
01

Definitions

In these terms: "YSP", "we", or "the organiser" means Youth for Sustainable Policy and its appointed Secretariat. "The conference" means YSP Winter MUN 2026, held virtually on 5–7 December 2026. "Delegate" or "you" means a person who applies for or is registered to participate. "Materials" means background guides, the Rules of Procedure primer, and other documents provided for the conference.

02

Eligibility & registration

Participation is open to individuals aged 16 to 28 at the time of the conference. Delegates under the age of 18 must complete the parental- or guardian-consent section of the Liability Waiver before participating.

  • Registration is complete only when your seat is confirmed, the applicable fee is paid (or an approved waiver is in place), and the required agreements are accepted.
  • You agree to provide accurate information. Material misrepresentation may result in withdrawal of a placement without refund.
  • A confirmed seat is personal to you and may not be transferred without the Secretariat's written approval (see clause 5).
03

Fees & payment

The registration fee is SGD 35 per individual delegate, or SGD 28 per delegate for school delegations of three or more. Fees are payable via PayNow (Singapore), bank transfer, or PayPal once your seat is confirmed.

  • Your placement is held for seven days from confirmation; payment (or finalisation of an approved waiver) must be completed within this window or the seat may be released.
  • Receipts are issued within 48 hours of payment.
  • Financial assistance — partial and full waivers are available. No delegate is turned away for financial reasons, and waiver status is confidential.
04

Cancellation & refunds

If you withdraw, the following applies based on the date we receive written notice at the conference email:

  • On or before 25 November 2026 — an 80% refund of fees paid.
  • After 25 November 2026 — fees are non-refundable, as materials and placements have been committed.
  • If YSP cancels the conference in full, registered delegates receive a full refund of fees paid (see clause 12 on force majeure).
05

Placements & substitution

Committee, country, and portfolio assignments are made at the Secretariat's discretion, weighting your stated preferences, experience, and overall balance across committees. We aim to honour first choices but cannot guarantee any specific assignment.

  • Where a first-choice committee is full, you will be placed in your second choice and notified before confirmation.
  • Substitution of one delegate for another within a confirmed school delegation may be permitted with written approval, subject to materials already issued.
06

Conduct & participation

All delegates are bound by the YSP Code of Conduct and the conference Rules of Procedure for the duration of the event, including all associated channels and social sessions.

  • Harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, or disruptive behaviour may result in removal from the conference without refund.
  • This is a moderated youth space; the Secretariat may take action it considers necessary to keep it safe.
  • Decisions of the dais on procedure, and of the Secretariat on conduct, are final.
07

Platform & technical

The conference runs virtually over Zoom, Discord, and shared document tools. You are responsible for your own equipment, internet connection, and a suitable environment for participation.

  • YSP provides dedicated technical support during sessions but is not responsible for delegate-side connectivity, hardware, or third-party platform outages.
  • Access credentials are personal and must not be shared. Sharing access may result in removal.
08

Recording & media

Sessions may be recorded for delegate access, training, accessibility, and limited promotional use. By participating, you acknowledge that your name, voice, likeness, and on-screen contributions may be captured.

  • Detailed consent and opt-out provisions are set out in the Liability Waiver, including specific protections for delegates under 18.
  • Delegates may not record, screenshot, or redistribute other delegates' contributions without consent.
09

Intellectual property

Background guides, the ROP primer, and other YSP-produced materials remain the property of YSP and are licensed to you for personal conference use only. They may not be redistributed or published without permission.

You retain ownership of documents you author — position papers, working papers, resolutions, and directives — and grant YSP a non-exclusive licence to use them for the administration, evaluation, and documentation of the conference. See the YSP Intellectual Property Policy.

10

Data & privacy

Information you provide is used only to administer the conference — placement, communication, payment, and evaluation. It is handled in line with the YSP Data Privacy Policy and the Privacy Notice.

Financial-assistance status is confidential and is not disclosed to chairs or other delegates.

11

Awards & evaluation

Awards are determined by the dais using a structured rubric across documents authored, floor presence, bloc collaboration, and foreign-policy alignment. Evaluation is at the sole discretion of the dais and Secretariat, and outcomes are final and not subject to appeal.

12

Liability & force majeure

To the fullest extent permitted by law, YSP is not liable for indirect or consequential loss arising from participation, technical failure, or content shared by other delegates. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

Force majeure. If events beyond our reasonable control — including platform failure, public-health measures, or similar — require us to modify, postpone, or cancel the conference, we will act reasonably and communicate promptly. Full cancellation by YSP entitles registered delegates to a full refund of fees paid.
13

Changes to these terms

YSP may update these terms before the conference where reasonably necessary. Material changes will be communicated to registered delegates by email, and the version number and date above will be updated. Continued participation after notice constitutes acceptance.

14

Governing law & contact

These terms are governed by the laws of Singapore. Questions about registration or these terms can be sent to the Secretariat, who reply within 48 hours on weekdays.

Acceptance

By confirming a seat at YSP Winter MUN 2026, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions, the Code of Conduct, and the Delegate Liability Waiver.
