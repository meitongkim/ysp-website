01 / About the competition

Short briefs, real policy.

Participants submit a 600–900 word policy brief responding to a climate and energy policy prompt.

  • 01Develop research synthesis and policy argumentation skills
  • 02Introduce YSP’s publication and writing standards
  • 03Produce accessible, high-quality climate policy writing
02 / Theme & tracks

Fossil fuel subsidy reform.

The 2026 promptChoose one fossil fuel subsidy reform policy implemented in any country in the last five years. Evaluate its effectiveness and recommend one improvement a national government could adopt by 2030.

03 / Eligibility & rules

Submission Requirements

  • 01Length of 600–900 words.
  • 02Analyse one fossil fuel subsidy reform implemented in any country within the last five years.
  • 03Evaluate how effective that reform has been.
  • 04Recommend one improvement a national government could adopt by 2030.
  • 05Submit by 15 October 2026, 11:59 PM GMT+8.

08 / FAQ

Questions.

Can I still enter the 2026 competition?

No. Submissions closed on 15 October 2026 at 11:59 PM GMT+8, and results were announced on 5 November 2026. Register Interest for 2027 instead.

How long did a brief need to be?

Between 600 and 900 words.

How were briefs judged?

Each brief was scored from 1 to 10 on argument quality, evidence use, accessibility and policy practicality, for a total out of 40. Every finalist brief was read in full.
