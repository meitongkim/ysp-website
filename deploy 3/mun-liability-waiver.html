Delegate liability waiver.

A plain-language release that every delegate accepts at registration. Because YSP Winter MUN 2026 is fully virtual, the risks are limited — but participation still involves a release of liability, a media consent, and, for delegates under 18, a parental or guardian consent.

This waiver is a legal acknowledgement, not legal advice. Read it in full before accepting. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must review and complete Section 7 with you.
In consideration of being permitted to participate in YSP Winter MUN 2026, I (the "delegate") acknowledge and agree to the following on my own behalf and, where applicable, with my parent or guardian.
01

Nature of the event

YSP Winter MUN 2026 is a fully virtual conference conducted over Zoom, Discord, and shared online tools on 5–7 December 2026. Participation takes place from the delegate's own location using the delegate's own equipment and internet connection. There is no travel, in-person gathering, or physical venue organised by YSP.

02

Assumption of risk

I understand that participation in an online event carries certain inherent risks, and I voluntarily accept them, including but not limited to:

  • Technical risks — connectivity loss, hardware or software failure, and third-party platform outages.
  • Screen-based participation — extended screen time and associated fatigue or eye strain.
  • Online interaction — exposure to the conduct or content of other participants, despite active moderation.
  • Data transmission — the ordinary risks of communicating over the internet.

I confirm that I am participating voluntarily and am responsible for arranging a safe and suitable environment from which to take part.

03

Release of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, I release and hold harmless Youth for Sustainable Policy, its Secretariat, organisers, volunteers, and partners (the "released parties") from any claims, demands, or damages arising out of my participation, including those connected to:

  • Technical failures, interruptions, or loss of access during the conference;
  • The acts, omissions, or content of other delegates or third parties;
  • Reliance on conference materials or outcomes.

This release does not apply to liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

04

Technology & online safety

I am responsible for my own device, software, internet connection, and account security. I agree to keep access credentials private and not to share them. I understand YSP provides technical support during sessions but is not responsible for delegate-side equipment or connectivity, or for outages of third-party platforms.

05

Wellbeing & conduct

I agree to abide by the YSP Code of Conduct, the conference Rules of Procedure, and the Terms & Conditions throughout the event. I understand this is a moderated, safe space for youth, and that the Secretariat may remove a participant whose conduct compromises that space, without refund.

If I experience distress or a safety concern during the conference, I understand I can contact the Secretariat or a designated welfare contact through the conference channels at any time.

06

Media & image release

I understand that sessions may be recorded and that my name, voice, likeness, and on-screen contributions may be captured for delegate access, accessibility, training, and limited promotional use by YSP.

  • I grant YSP a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use such recordings for these purposes.
  • Opt-out — I may request not to be featured in promotional material by notifying the Secretariat in writing before the conference; this does not affect recordings made for delegate access.
  • For delegates under 18, media consent is subject to the parental or guardian consent in Section 7.
07

Consent for delegates under 18

Where the delegate is under 18 at the time of the conference, a parent or legal guardian must complete this section. By doing so, the parent or guardian confirms that they have read this waiver in full, consent to the delegate's participation, and agree to its terms on the delegate's behalf — including the assumption of risk (§2), release of liability (§3), and media and image release (§6).

A parent or guardian may withdraw a minor's media consent at any time by written notice, without affecting the delegate's participation in the conference.
08

Indemnification

I agree to indemnify the released parties against reasonable claims or losses arising from my own breach of the Code of Conduct, the Terms & Conditions, or this waiver, or from my unlawful or intentionally harmful conduct during the conference. This clause does not extend to ordinary good-faith participation in debate.

09

General & governing law

If any provision of this waiver is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full effect. This waiver is governed by the laws of Singapore and should be read together with the conference Terms & Conditions and the YSP Code of Conduct.

Acknowledgement & acceptance

What you're agreeing to

  • I have read and understood this Liability Waiver in full.
  • I voluntarily assume the risks described in Section 2.
  • I agree to the release of liability and media release in Sections 3 and 6.
  • If under 18, my parent or guardian has completed Section 7 below.
