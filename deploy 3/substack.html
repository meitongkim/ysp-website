About this publication

Young people aren't future stakeholders. They're already shaping policy.

“Youth for Sustainable Policy was built on a simple belief: that young people are not just future stakeholders in policy — they are already shaping it. This organization exists to make policy accessible, rigorous, and actionable for our generation.”

Youth for Sustainable Policy (YSP) is a youth-led global organization dedicated to making public policy — particularly in sustainability, climate, and economic systems — accessible, rigorous, and relevant to young people.

We are driven by a multidisciplinary team of students and young professionals from across the world, including Singapore, India, Ukraine, the United Kingdom, and the United States, with academic backgrounds spanning law, politics, economics, finance, and international relations.

We translate complex policy issues into clear, structured, and intellectually grounded content — enabling young people not only to understand systems, but to question, critique, and contribute to them.
