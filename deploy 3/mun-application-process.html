The application process.

Six steps, about five minutes to start, and no account required. Here is exactly what happens from the moment you submit your application to the moment you take your seat on Day 1 — including every deadline and what we ask you to prepare.

Applications close15 November 2026
Placements confirmed25 November 2026
EligibilityAges 16–28, worldwide
Step by step

From application to placard.

Nothing here is a surprise. Every delegate moves through the same six steps, and the Secretariat is reachable at every one of them.

1
Now – 15 Nov Open

Submit your application

Complete the short delegate application on the MUN page. It takes about five minutes and asks for your details, your first- and second-choice committees, your MUN experience level, and whether you'd like to be considered for financial assistance.

  • No account or payment is required to apply.
  • School delegations of three or more can apply together for the group rate.
  • Financial-assistance requests are handled confidentially and never shared with chairs.
2
Within 48h Automatic

Receive your acknowledgement

You'll get an email confirming we've received your application, within 48 hours. It includes your reference number and a summary of your committee choices so you can check everything is correct. If anything is wrong, you simply reply.

3
By 25 Nov Placement

Get your committee placement

The Secretariat reviews applications and confirms placements by 25 November. We weight your first choice heavily, then balance experience level and country representation across each committee.

  • If your first choice is full, we place you in your second choice and write to you first.
  • Country and portfolio assignments are sent with your placement email.
4
Within 7 days of placement Action

Confirm your seat & register

To lock in your placement, confirm your seat and complete registration within seven days. This is where you agree to the conference terms and the delegate liability waiver, and pay your fee (or finalise your approved waiver).

  • Fees are paid via PayNow (SG), bank transfer, or PayPal. Receipts issue within 48 hours.
  • Review the Terms & Conditions and Liability Waiver before confirming.
  • Delegates under 18 complete the parental-consent section of the waiver.
5
8 – 28 Nov Prepare

Receive your prep pack & submit your position paper

Once confirmed, your delegate portal unlocks: your committee background guide, the Rules of Procedure primer, and your Discord access. Read your guide, then write and submit your position paper by 28 November.

  • Background guides release on 8 November.
  • Position papers are due 28 November, 23:59 SGT — one to two pages, with citations.
  • First-time delegates can request light written feedback on their draft.
6
5 – 7 Dec Conference

Take your seat

Join the opening ceremony on 5 December and step into three days of debate. You'll already know the platform, your committee, and your position — so you can spend the conference doing diplomacy, not figuring out logistics.

Before you apply

Who can apply, and what to have ready.

The application is deliberately light. You don't need anything formal to start — but knowing these things in advance makes it faster.

Eligibility

  • Ages 16–28 at the time of the conference. Delegates under 18 complete a parental-consent section.
  • Open worldwide. The schedule runs on SGT (UTC+8) to suit Asia, the Middle East, and Europe.
  • All experience levels. The Youth Assembly is built for first-timers; advanced committees suit experienced delegates.
  • No nomination needed. You can apply as an individual or as part of a school delegation.

Have these to hand

  • Your name, email, and country/region — that's all the application requires.
  • Your first and second committee choices (see Committee Topics if you're undecided).
  • An honest sense of your MUN experience level so we place you well.
  • A stable internet connection, microphone, and quiet space for the conference days.

The dates that matter.

Ready when you are

Start your application.

It takes about five minutes, and you can review the terms and waiver before you ever pay. Seats are placed in the order strong applications arrive.
